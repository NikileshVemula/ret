Rheumatology Services

Specialist assessment, education and management for autoimmune and inflammatory conditions in Trinidad & Tobago.

Autoimmune Arthritis

Rheumatoid Arthritis

A chronic autoimmune condition where the immune system attacks the joint lining, causing inflammation, pain, swelling and stiffness. Untreated, it leads to permanent joint damage and disability — making early diagnosis critical.

Joint Pain & SwellingMorning StiffnessFatigueSymmetrical JointsRheumatoid Nodules
  • Early DMARDs (methotrexate) to prevent joint damage
  • Biologic therapies for refractory or severe disease
  • Anti-inflammatory diet and lifestyle modification
  • Physiotherapy and joint protection strategies

Systemic Autoimmune Disease

Lupus (SLE)

A multi-system autoimmune disease affecting skin, joints, kidneys, brain, lungs and heart. Prevalent in women of childbearing age and common in T&T. Follows a relapsing-remitting course.

Butterfly RashPhotosensitivityArthritisRenal InvolvementSerositisHair Loss
  • Hydroxychloroquine as disease-modifying therapy
  • Organ-specific immunosuppression (cyclophosphamide, MMF)
  • Sun protection and lifestyle counselling
  • Monitoring of organ function and disease activity

Crystal Arthropathy

Gout

Caused by monosodium urate crystal deposition due to hyperuricaemia. Presents with sudden, intensely painful arthritis — most classically the first metatarsophalangeal joint. Untreated leads to tophi, deformities and kidney stones.

Acute Severe PainErythema & WarmthSwellingTophiKidney Stones
  • Urate-lowering therapy — allopurinol, febuxostat
  • Acute flare: colchicine, NSAIDs, corticosteroids
  • Dietary guidance: reduce purines, red meat, alcohol
  • Hydration and weight management advice

Degenerative Joint Disease

Osteoarthritis

The most common form of arthritis — progressive cartilage degradation most frequently affecting knees, hips, hands and lumbar spine. Prevalence rises with age, obesity and prior joint injury.

Joint Pain with ActivityStiffness <30minCrepitusReduced Range of MotionBony Enlargement
  • Physiotherapy, exercise prescription and posture correction
  • Weight management and footwear advice
  • Analgesics and topical anti-inflammatory agents
  • Intra-articular injections where indicated

Inflammatory Arthritis

Psoriatic Arthritis

Inflammatory arthritis affecting up to 30% of people with psoriasis. Can involve peripheral joints, spine, entheses and tendons. Nail involvement and dactylitis are characteristic. Classified using CASPAR criteria.

Psoriasis PlaquesNail PittingDactylitisEnthesitisSacroiliitis
  • DMARDs: methotrexate, leflunomide, sulfasalazine
  • Biologics: TNF-inhibitors, IL-17 inhibitors
  • Coordinated dermatology and rheumatology management
  • Physical therapy and enthesitis management

Vasculitis & CTDs

Includes Sjogren's Disease, Systemic Sclerosis, Idiopathic Inflammatory Myopathies, Giant Cell Arteritis and ANCA-associated vasculitis. Each requires specialist assessment and tailored treatment.
